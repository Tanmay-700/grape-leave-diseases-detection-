4. Mathematical Rigor & Formulation Audit

All ten equations in the research paper have been re-verified against published literature and our Python/PyTorch codebase:

  • Equation (1) - Excess Green Index: Verified exact match with segment.py: 2*g - r - b > 0.
  • Equation (2) - Foliage Prior Mask: Verified exact match with HSV color filtering in segment.py: cv2.inRange(hsv, (18, 20, 20), (95, 255, 255)).
  • Equations (3) & (4) - Centroid & Bounding Box: Verified exact match with np.median(xs) and np.percentile(xs, [1, 99]).
  • Equations (5) & (6) - Cosine Annealing & Label Smoothing: Verified exact match with torch.optim.lr_scheduler.CosineAnnealingLR and nn.CrossEntropyLoss(label_smoothing=0.1).
  • Equation (7) - AdamW Parameter Update: Verified exact match with decoupled weight decay ($\lambda = 10^{-4}$).
  • Equation (8) - Weighted Sampling: Verified exact match with WeightedRandomSampler applying $2.0\times$ boost for outdoor natural images.
  • Equation (9) - Ensemble Soft Voting: Verified exact match with probability blending in classify.py: (results[0]['probabilities'] + results[1]['probabilities']) / 2.0.
  • Equation (10) - Grad-CAM Formulation: Verified exact match with gradient global average pooling and ReLU activation in gradcam.py.
